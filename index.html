About the Event

RomantasyCon Los Angeles is a three-day celebration of fantasy romance featuring bestselling authors, interactive workshops, cosplay, exclusive merchandise, book signings, and unforgettable experiences for readers of every kind.

What to Expect

  • 📚 Meet Bestselling Authors: Attend signings, Q&A sessions, and exclusive panels.
  • ⚔ Fantasy Activities: Participate in workshops, themed games, and giveaways.
  • 👑 Dress to Impress: Join the Warrior Princess Ball in your favorite fantasy attire.
  • 🛍 Vendor Hall: Browse books, artwork, jewelry, and handmade merchandise.

Event Schedule

📜 Writing Seminar Panel 📜

Join authors Breanne Randall, Liz Zander, and Adina Chiles to discuss how to write the first chapter. The struggles, the format, the process, everything you need to know if you are feeling lost and don't know where to start.

💎 Jewelry Making Workshop 💎

Join us in our jewelry making workshop. We will learn how to make our own set of earrings and a bracelet from your choice of jewels, pearls, and gems.

👸 Fantasy Ball 🤴

As a fun goodbye to this year's RomantasyCon Los Angeles, we are hosting our Romantasy Ball. This year's theme is Warrior Princess. Dress up! Dance! Have fun!

⚔ Sword Fighting Demonstration ⚔

Watch professional stunt performers demonstrate medieval swordplay techniques inspired by your favorite fantasy novels. Learn the basics of safe stage combat, discover the history behind iconic weapons, and even volunteer to try a few beginner moves under instructor guidance.

☕ Fantasy Tea Party ☕

Step into an enchanted garden for an afternoon tea filled with magical pastries, specialty teas, and fantasy-inspired decorations. Meet fellow readers, participate in book discussions, and enjoy themed games and trivia while dressed in your favorite fantasy attire.

🧚‍♀️ Character Cosplay Contest 🧝

Show off your creativity by dressing as your favorite romantasy character for a chance to win exclusive prizes. Judges will score contestants on craftsmanship, creativity, and stage presence before crowning this year's Cosplay Champion.
